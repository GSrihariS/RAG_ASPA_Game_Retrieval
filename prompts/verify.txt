Use exactly one verdict:

- contradicted: cited evidence directly opposes the core assertion (for example 'stable performance' versus reports of crashes).

An exact quote alone does not establish entailment. Check full cited text for negation, sarcasm, caveats and conditions; do not use external knowledge.
