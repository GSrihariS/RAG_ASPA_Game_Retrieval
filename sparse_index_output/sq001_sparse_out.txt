4. steam:1903340:237072225:chunk:001 | Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 | score=3.9263
Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 knows how to make an entrance. Its opening is beautiful, uncomfortable and quietly devastating, establishing a world where everyone lives with an expiration date. That premise could easily have carried a straightforward adventure about stopping a mysterious enemy. Thankfully, the game has far more on its mind.

The story is its strongest point. Beneath the expedition and the unanswered questions lies a deeply personal story about loss and belonging. What do you do with grief when there is nowhere to put it? How much of yourself becomes tied to the people you love? These questions give the journey weight without turning every conversation into a philosophical speech. Sometimes that weight comes through in a character struggling to say something. Sometimes it comes through in what they choose to avoid altogether.
