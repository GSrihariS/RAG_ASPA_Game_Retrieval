Setting and narative tone

Fantasy settings are much easier to construct, as almost anything can be explained/brushed aside and help suspension of disbelief settle in. RDR2 goes for a historical setting and manages to deliver something close to a documentary.
The overall tone of the game is also rather dark and mature. Life in the game is tough and unforgiving, the protagonists and their crew are morally gray at best. The game also features classic Rockstar social commentary, with a notable part dedicated to native american struggles.
The overarching theme of times changing and leaving some people behind is presented really well, supported significantly by side content and environmental queues.

It's a 12/10 game really, and a benchmark tthat even Rockstar themselves will find extremely hard to beat.
