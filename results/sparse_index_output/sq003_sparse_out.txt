5. steam:1091500:236864903:chunk:003 | Cyberpunk 2077 | score=2.5917
You have a limited calendar and have to decide how to spend it. Do you improve a relationship? Work on yourself? Explore the palace? Prepare for the next major confrontation? All of these systems are asking the player some variation of the same question. What are you going to do with the time you have? And Cyberpunk presents one of the strongest possible versions of that question. When you're dying, what do you do with your time?

Apparently nothing. Get up samurai, we're going drag racing. Get up samurai, we're clearing every gig and side mission on the entire map. Get up samurai, we're going bowling. This is the massive elephant that I don't think gets criticized nearly enough. The story presents V with a very simple and horrifying problem. You are dying.
