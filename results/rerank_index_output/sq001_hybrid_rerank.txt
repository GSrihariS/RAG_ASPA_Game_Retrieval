The characters fit nicely into this world visually speaking, even if the monologues and dialogue might be a little cheesy at times.

The gameplay itself is somewhat interesting, the need to adapt and build synergy between the characters keeps the repetitive gameplay loop bearable. Enemy encounters are usually very similar or identical, even among different enemy groups. The battles themselves are enjoyable, as you need to be accurate with your timings while attacking, dodging, and parrying. Overall, the system repeats itself heavily, which becomes painfully evident in Act II. The story is still there, but it’s basically just a copy and paste of Act I, minus the new scenery.

One thing we also “get to have” whilst progressing through the game is new companions with their own abilities and powers. While it’s great to give the player more options to “choose” from, the game doesn't actually let you choose much.
