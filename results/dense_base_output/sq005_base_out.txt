The biggest problem

The frustrating part is that Silksong clearly knows how to make fantastic difficult gameplay.

When the game gives me a boss, a short runback and a readable moveset, I can happily die ten times and keep thinking:

“One more try.”

When the game gives me a long enemy gauntlet, platforming, environmental hazards and no checkpoint, I start thinking:

“Why am I wasting my time doing this again?”

That distinction ultimately killed the game for me.

I don't mind difficult games. I don't even mind extremely difficult games. I mind difficulty created by making me repeat content I have already mastered.

I eventually reached a section with an enemy arena dealing two-mask damage, followed by a lava escape/platforming sequence, without a meaningful checkpoint. At that point I simply stopped playing.
