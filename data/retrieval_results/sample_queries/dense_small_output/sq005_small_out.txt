There is always that feeling that something is hidden just around the corner, and exploration constantly rewards curiosity.

The world feels mysterious without simply copying Hollow Knight. It has its own personality, its own history, and plenty of things left unexplained for the player to discover.

Combat & Bosses

The combat is one of the biggest strengths of the game.

Hornet's speed and movement make fights much more aggressive and dynamic. The bosses are varied, memorable, and often require you to properly learn their patterns instead of simply attacking until one of you stops moving.

Personally, I didn't find the game particularly difficult. There were certainly challenging encounters, but the difficulty never felt excessive or unfair to me. Once I understood the patterns and adapted to the fights, most of them became very satisfying to overcome.
