Thoroughly enjoyed Cyberpunk 2077. I chose to purchase it well after the bugs and customer complaints which were coming out during its initial release, meaning I've had minimal issues.

Interesting storyline, fun side quests/missions, some in-depth characters and a strong performance from Keanu Reeves. As someone who loves character customisation, this game allows for some pretty fantastic outfit, hair, makeup, facial feature and weapon options.

I recommend this, especially if you're a fan of futuristic, dystopian cities and challenging morals.
