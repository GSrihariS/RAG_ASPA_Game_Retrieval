I like it, rating it 4/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐. A lot busier than the other Dawn of War titles. Feels like you're commanding an actual big army this time. Also base-building is improved compared to Dawn of War 2, but still not as robust as OG Dawn of War. Audio and voice overs are great too. Makes you feel the urgency.

Not 5 star because of the cluttered UI. It takes some getting used to but needlessly complicated.
