Text:
They basically made Hades again, but sacrificed some of the character and story depth for making the gameplay even more addictive. As good if not better than the original.

I remember loading up this game for the first time and feeling disappointed with the magic system since I thought it would take away from the gameplay's adrenaline. In fact it just make the gameplay feel more tactical; there are immediately twice as many layers and possibilities for builds. Which is only made better by having two distinct paths for progression!

There's not much to say about the audio-visual pieces, since the art and music are iterated from the original Hades and are similarly awesome. But yeah this game is great, and you will not regret playing it (I don't think you need to play the original but it definitely helps!)
