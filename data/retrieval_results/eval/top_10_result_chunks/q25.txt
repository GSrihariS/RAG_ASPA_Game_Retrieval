Honestly, after waiting for what felt like forever, stepping into Pharloom as Hornet was worth every single second. I’m definitely not the most skilled gamer out there I died way more times than I’d like to admit but Hollow Knight: Silksong is an absolute masterpiece.

The movement feels so fluid and acrobatic compared to the original. Jumping, sprinting, and using Hornet’s needle craft makes combat feel like a fast-paced dance. The handcrafted art style, atmospheric music, and intricate world design continually blew me away. Every new area had me completely immersed, even when a brutal boss had me stuck for an hour.

It’s tough, but every victory feels earned and ridiculously satisfying. If you enjoy action platformers, atmospheric worlds, or just a really solid challenge, I can't recommend this game enough. Do yourself a favor and give it a play you won't regret it!
