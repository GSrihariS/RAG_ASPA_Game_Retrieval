Honestly, after waiting for what felt like forever, stepping into Pharloom as Hornet was worth every single second. I’m definitely not the most skilled gamer out there I died way more times than I’d like to admit but Hollow Knight: Silksong is an absolute masterpiece.

The movement feels so fluid and acrobatic compared to the original. Jumping, sprinting, and using Hornet’s needle craft makes combat feel like a fast-paced dance.
