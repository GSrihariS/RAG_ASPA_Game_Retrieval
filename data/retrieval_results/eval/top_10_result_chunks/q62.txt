I loved RE7. RE8, however, was massively overhyped. Thankfully, RE9 is a return to form.

Let's start with the positives.

+It looks great.
+Runs well and is properly optimised.
+Crunchier, more satisfying combat with much better weapon sounds.
+More memorable than RE8, with stronger writing.
+An overall far more enjoyable experience than RE8.

Now the negatives.

-Grace's voice acting gets annoying with her constant stammering.
-Leon's voice actor sounds bored at times, although I suppose that's just part of his badass persona?
-Nerfed dismemberment compared to previous games.
-Still no f*****g FOV slider. Seriously, Capcom?

Overall, RE9 is a massive improvement over RE8. Better combat, better presentation, better writing, and a much more memorable experience. It's not perfect, but Capcom cooked - mostly.
