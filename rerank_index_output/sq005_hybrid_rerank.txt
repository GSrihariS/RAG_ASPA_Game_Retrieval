We also need to talk about skill and difficulty.

Difficulty alone is not what makes games like Dark Souls or Elden Ring (and indeed, the first Hollow Knight game) good. Those games are specifically designed to give the player very straight-forward ways to compensate for lack of mechanical skill.

They make sure that the player has some way to increase their character power and then come back to a difficult fight if the player's skill alone is insufficient. This is a good thing, because not every player possesses very high mechanical skill or is interested in grinding the same boss for 5 hours. It allows for alternative ways of solving problems and makes the game more interesting. It allows "exploiting" the game more which is a separate skillset from plain input execution.
